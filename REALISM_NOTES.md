# Realism notes and vertical-slice boundaries

The synthetic playable traffic scenario is named **2026 Current**, anchored to 2026-09-06. Selected airport surface facts use Taiwan CAA AIRAC 05/26, effective 2026-10-29, while the flight scenario retains its separate date anchor. This is a research-backed game prototype, not a complete replay of airport operations on either date. Research and date conflicts are documented in DATA_SOURCES.md.

## Geometry and routing

Runway physical dimensions, declared distances and displaced thresholds were verified. The current AIP stand-coordinate table supplies 93 positions; selected taxiway intersections and those published stand coordinates were projected into local metres. The tangent-plane origin is the north runway midpoint, shifted to z=-760; x follows the 049.08° true runway axis. Local z points southeast and y is height. The south runway is reduced to a exactly parallel centerline, a negligible visual simplification over airport scale. Render headings are radians: +x is π/2, −z is 0. Runway magnetic designations remain 05/23; true bearing and scene heading are distinct quantities.

The navigable graph preserves selected published taxiway relationships. It is not a random road network, but it is a reduced network: some high-speed exits, outlying maintenance and north-side cargo taxiways are absent. Rapid-exit bends, fillets, pavement flares, stand lead-ins, pushback turning, holding offsets and gate approach curves are simplified. The main runways are not graph shortcuts. Aircraft enter through explicit hold/entry state transitions, and arrival exits connect back to the graph.

Taxiway names belong to **edges**. Keys such as N12/N13, apron-D5, hold-north-05 and entry-south-23 are internal identifiers and must not be presented as real taxiway designations. The legacy NC/SC naming has not been substituted for the current N/P/Q/R/S system. W1/W2 are independent parallel western connectors; the narrower W linking segment and its 36 m wingspan restriction are recorded separately. Apron access labels are descriptive game labels, not invented taxiway names.

Taxiway widths use published principal dimensions where available, with conservative defaults on reduced connectors; the graph does not model all section-specific pavement widths or shoulders. Maximum wingspan settings are simulation rules because current AIP stand MAX ACFT entries are NIL. Adjacent stand dependency, full jet-blast envelopes and exact tug escape paths are not validated. The supported fleet stays below 65 m span; Code F aircraft cannot be added without the restrictions in SUP19/26.

Terminal main blocks, concourse widths/heights, roads, maintenance buildings, tower shape and decorative infrastructure are approximate original geometry. The new tower structural height is supported by ANWS, but cab floor height, glazing, furniture and workstation positions are invented functional interiors. No tower room dimensions or proprietary ATC display layout are claimed as exact. T3 north/D11–D18 is included; main and south T3 remain construction. The precise amount of work completed on the reference day is unknown.

## Operations and time

Ground/Tower lifecycle states, command validity, graph routing, runway occupancy, handoffs and safety consequences are simulation systems. AI assistance can accelerate unattended positions. Taxi/radio/engine delays are condensed to make complete flights playable. Arrival vectors and departure handoffs represent local gameplay, not the published SID/STAR/ILS procedures. The slice does not reproduce airspace sector boundaries, certified approach minima, phraseology compliance or controller staffing procedures.

Wake-category timing and separation triggers are conservative game abstractions. Incursion, conflict and collision scoring provide consequences, not legal findings. Runway protected-area logic cannot be used to assess real separation. Ordinary inspection closures are retained as metadata but are not automatically imposed. Live NOTAMs, runway condition reports, worksite changes, crane obstacle surfaces, fireworks windows and ATFM restrictions are not synchronized. The SUP25/22 extension discrepancy remains explicitly unresolved.

Aircraft are deterministic kinematic models. Different dimensions, reference weights, accelerations, taxi speeds, rotation speeds, braking and climb profiles produce distinct behavior. Weight and weather influence the model within bounded formulas. It does not calculate engine-specific thrust tables, lift/drag polars, certified balanced-field lengths, true tire friction, brake-energy limits, flap scheduling or aircraft flight-manual V-speeds. Reference/empty weights and V1/VR values are representative parameters; specific airline weight variants are not verified.

Traffic is a **Typical Taoyuan Traffic Day** scenario fixture. Airline/type pairings reflect researched fleets; flight numbers, destinations, times and aircraft assignments are fictional training records unless a future imported provider supplies verified records. No registration identifies a fabricated real aircraft. Date selection and start time control scenario timing; they do not guarantee a historical timetable or a different certified airport configuration. Only the 2026 geometry is playable. `airportGeometryByEra` and `airportOperationalRules` remain separate for later dated adapters.

Weather presets are generated scenario conditions, not observations from the selected day. Lighting and rain/cloud/fog effects support visibility and atmosphere; they are not a full atmospheric scattering, precipitation or wind-field simulation. Day/night time follows scenario clock. Ground services and people are gameplay animation systems, not full airport logistics agents.

Brand sources and rights are recorded separately from the locally bundled Poly Haven CC0 concrete/asphalt and sky assets. The sky image supplies cloud detail and reflections while scenario time/weather still control atmosphere and wetness; it is not a photograph of RCTP. No permission for public/commercial redistribution of third-party logos is assumed. See AIRFRAME_UPDATE.md, LIVERY_UPDATE.md and ASSET_PROVENANCE.md.

The subsequent aircraft/airport detail pass adds intake lining, visible fans, door/hatch detailing and apron equipment. Thirty-six equipment areas are buffered from the reduced graph's straight centreline segments; they are visual scenery and do not participate in collision or routing. The red/white boundaries, gate signs, floodlight fittings and two wind-responsive windsocks are original approximations, not surveyed positions or operational guidance. The graph buffer does not establish clearance for every smoothed turn. See AIRPORT_DETAIL_UPDATE.md.

Before upgrading fidelity, obtain dated airport restrictions and a reliable timetable export, validate the complete taxi topology and stand limits, replace approximate building footprints with licensed survey/photogrammetry data, and add manufacturer airport-planning performance tables. Keep facts with source/publication/effective/expiry/verification metadata. Never automatically enable published-but-not-yet-effective configurations.

## Latest playable-slice details

Random weather scenarios evolve wind and conditions after eight simulated minutes, then at seeded six-to-eleven-minute intervals. Fixed presets retain their selected conditions except for optional weather incidents or explicit controls. Wind recommendation is advisory; it never instantly reverses committed aircraft. Tutorial arrivals are released after the departure handoff, avoiding time pressure while learning. Aircraft that finish docking remain visible at their occupied stand for three simulated minutes before being recycled. Attached pushback tugs and animated marshaller figures are visual service approximations; jet-bridge reach and docking are simplified. The HUD includes a 93-position stand occupancy board and a workload indicator.

## Ground intelligence and planning — 2026-10-27

Surface reservations are conservative game resources derived from the existing graph and aircraft envelopes. They model single-lane corridors, junctions, apron maneuver space, runway entry and generic runway crossings. They are not published stop-bar positions, certified wingtip clearances or a full airport surface movement guidance system. A complete corridor may be held for one aircraft even where a more detailed surveyed model could support greater capacity.

The current reduced airport graph contains runway entry links, but no through taxi route crossing an active runway. Crossing clearance and runway protection are implemented and tested on a deterministic crossing fixture; route alternatives truthfully report crossings from their actual graph edges. Adding operational RCTP crossings requires verified topology and restrictions. No crossing was invented to create difficulty.

Departure opportunities and wake timers predict the simulator's kinematic model. Roll, response, landing exit and wake intervals remain game approximations. Estimates can change with weather, queueing or a blocked exit. They do not certify that a real aircraft can use a gap. Standard supplies operational information without a persistent safe/unsafe answer indicator; command-time warnings and physical safety guards still apply.

Occasional inbound gate pressure, traffic-bank intensity and difficulty are synthetic operational scenarios. Gate compatibility uses the existing provisional span limits. Gate planning does not imply validated passenger connections, airline stand ownership, jet-blast clearances or exact adjacent stand procedures. Recycled completed aircraft follow the existing three-minute stand representation; blocked aircraft are never recycled as a deadlock remedy.

Direction changes can be planned while traffic is active. The simulator pauses new scheduled demand and departure taxi clearances, drains committed traffic, then applies the change. This is a playable simplified transition, not a reconstruction of real airport runway configuration procedures. Controller / Hardcore retains emergency prevention and internal-cycle recovery; optional severe collision presentation is not added by this pass.
