Real-world airline branding — personal game configuration

Airline names, marks, wordmarks and livery identities remain the property of
their respective rights holders. This game configuration uses them to depict
real-world aircraft at the user's request for a personal, noncommercial game.
No airline endorsement or permission for commercial use or public redistribution
is claimed. Noncommercial use alone does not establish permission.

The local sources, source-page URLs, owners, rights labels, byte counts and
SHA-256 digests are recorded in manifest.json. The THAI tail orchid is a
symbol-only derivative of the cited THAI logo vector; the complete lockup stays
on the fuselage. No airline artwork is fetched at runtime; the game composes
local vectors into its own existing aircraft UV maps.

The four Taiwan-core carrier identities continue to use their original local
assets. Existing vector artwork also covers Air India, Cathay Pacific, Singapore
Airlines, Korean Air and Emirates. This update adds local vector marks for Thai
Airways, Turkish Airlines, Japan Airlines, Air New Zealand, ANA, KLM and Peach.

Source pages label the cited Commons logos as public-domain simple text/logo
artwork. The Japan Airlines Tsurumaru SVG is from Simple Icons, whose artwork
is CC0. Those labels apply only to the respective source files; trademarks and
other brand rights remain separate. Tigerair Taiwan and STARLUX artwork comes
from their official websites and is not described as CC0 or openly licensed.

The China Airlines plum blossom is the original embedded PNG extracted without
resampling from its source SVG. That source page labels the artwork Fair use;
this is recorded as a source label, not as a transferable license or a claim
that this game qualifies for the same exception.

Peach's current aircraft-era livery is shown with its older aircraft wordmark.
Peach says the new aircraft design is planned for spring 2027; the new 2026
brand mark is therefore not substituted onto the pre-refresh aircraft livery.

Reference photos and official airline pages are used for visual research only;
they are not bundled as runtime textures. Tail marks and wordmarks are local
vectors, while paint placement is adapted to the game's existing simplified
aircraft UVs. This is not a registration-specific manufacturer paint kit.
