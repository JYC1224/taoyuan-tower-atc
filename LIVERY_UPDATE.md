# Real-world standard liveries — 2026-10-28

The four airlines now use genuine brand wordmarks/emblems and individually reconstructed standard paint layouts on the existing aircraft. This replaces the common color band and EVA/CI/STARLUX text previously used on the tails. The user's stated use is a game that will not be sold.

| Airline | Result |
| --- | --- |
| EVA Air | EVA AIR wordmark; globe/compass tail emblem; green fin/belly and orange boundary |
| China Airlines | CHINA AIRLINES wordmark; real plum blossom on a pale fin, without the earlier CI tail initials |
| STARLUX | STARLUX wordmark; real S/Polaris emblem; white fin, gold boundary, dark belly/engines, belly wordmark |
| Tigerair Taiwan | tigerair taiwan wordmark; yellow/black tiger-pattern fin; yellow engines and tips |

Both tail faces use separate atlas charts so asymmetric emblems are not reversed. The text is readable on both fuselage sides. The CAL emblem's paper background is composited into the existing paint rather than rendered as a rectangular sticker. Logo images are decoded once locally and baked into the existing 4096×1024 fuselage / 1024×1024 fin maps. Retired textures are not repainted after asynchronous decode.

This is an improvement in real-world identity, not an exact manufacturer's paint kit. Placement, color fields, tiger stripes and the procedural aircraft geometry remain game reconstructions. Individual registration numbers and special-event/character liveries are not introduced.

## Sources and rights

Seven small local brand files total **55,110 bytes**. Their URLs, owners, copyright labels and SHA-256 digests are recorded in `public/assets/liveries/manifest.json`; `NOTICE.txt` accompanies the build. EVA identity and the EVA/CAL/STARLUX wordmarks use the specific Commons source files listed there. STARLUX's S/Polaris emblem and Tigerair Taiwan's wordmark come from official websites. The CAL plum blossom is the original embedded PNG extracted without resampling from the Chinese Wikipedia SVG source. That source labels its use Fair use; this is recorded as a source label, not transferable permission for this game.

Official [EVA 787-9](https://www.evaair.com/en-global/fly-prepare/our-fleets/passenger-airplanes/787-9/), [STARLUX A350-900](https://www.starlux-airlines.com/en-Global/experience/walk-into-starlux/our-fleet/a350-900), [STARLUX identity](https://www.starlux-airlines.com/en-Global/about-us/knowing-us), [Tigerair fleet](https://www.tigerairtw.com/zh-tw/about-tigerair/carrier-info) and manufacturer delivery material were inspected for the standard schemes. Photographs and website code used for research are not runtime/release assets.

Brand art is **not all CC0 or open licensed**, unlike the seven environment images. Rights remain with their owners; this integration does not claim airline authorization or endorsement. Noncommercial intent alone does not automatically establish permission or fair use, as [TIPO explains](https://www.tipo.gov.tw/tw/copyright/692-16335.html). No determination of a copyright exception for public/commercial redistribution is made here.
