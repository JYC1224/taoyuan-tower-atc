principal space before modeling it. References were viewed in Codex's in-app
browser. Reference photographs are not included in the game. Photographs from

## Modeled allocation and limits

- The old A wing was a 660 × 120 m ground-level glass hall, extending through
  several projected aircraft stands. Its replacement is an authored 660 × 40 m
  lounge/service wing on the terminal side of the stands, with a distinct 11 m
  upper passage. An aircraft-envelope regression checks the stand clearance.
  Width/depth are scene fits; they are not measured airport dimensions.
- The upper passage meets the existing T1 3F deck at elevation 10.94. The
  lounge deck uses the existing scene elevation 5.44 to express the split-level
  photographs and join the boarding bridges. **This does not establish that the
  airport officially labels these rooms 2F.** Their menu labels avoid that claim.
  Current public maps show them in the departure program; no verified measured
  section establishes their exact elevations. The lower service storey is an
  exterior mass, not a claimed interior reconstruction.
- A1–A3 share one room; A7 has a separate photo-led structural treatment.
  Remaining A rooms share this broad structural section with neutral walls.
  Their individual artwork and complete fit-outs are not claimed to match.
- Mountain and blossom colour studies are original procedural illustrations,
  not scans or exact reproductions of the airport's commissioned artwork.
  The passage's short cream-lettering panels are newly typeset interpretations
  of the dark calligraphy-wall colour/design field; they do not reproduce the
  artist's handwriting or complete poem.
- The 3F main terminal joins the A-side passage through the central portal.
  Both stairs in each modeled waiting room now connect to the upper passage.
  Walking follows the stair pitch while glass guards retain the exposed edges.
  The metal and glass landing detail follows the separate A8 stair and
  overhead waiting-room views in the [A-side photo essay](https://eric218.pixnet.net/blog/posts/8185478879).
- New seat banks, round columns, core and stairs share layout data with walking
  collision. Floor textures use metre-based UVs. Seats, reveals, mullions,
  louvered luminaires and handrails are instanced; local fill lights activate
  only inside their inspection spaces. Existing quality settings are retained.

This is a documented architectural improvement, not a complete measured replica
of T1/T2. Remaining priority: obtain matching current multi-angle views for T2
airside corridors, immigration/security and arrival routes before rebuilding
their large volumes. Brands and shop inventories remain secondary.
