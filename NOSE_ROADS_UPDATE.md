# Nose and landside accuracy update — 2026-10-28

The cockpit was previously drawn only on the sides of the fuselage; from directly ahead it appeared blank, and all families had the same pane layout. The new procedural, mirrored glazing reaches the front centreline. Family-specific nose length, rounded profile and pane outlines separate the A320/A321, A330, A350, 787 and 777; 787 uses two panes per side and the A350 has its dark surround. These are visual interpretations of photographs, not certified manufacturer models. The existing aircraft liveries, engines and other details are retained.

The airport no longer uses fictional straight perimeter roads or the two artificial roadside parking rectangles. Mapped apron polygons and parking outlines fill areas previously shown as grass, while the grass between the runways and taxiways remains. A selected road network, including Terminal North and South Roads, is projected into the same coordinate system as the existing airport. Tunnel/covered segments are suppressed; visible surface roads are conservatively clipped against the game's runway and aircraft-taxi corridors. Roads are visual scenery, not aircraft routes. Parking bay markings, lane widths without reliable tags, terminal buildings and the rest of the surrounding landscape remain approximate.

The source data are **© OpenStreetMap contributors**; the attribution links to https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and the sanitized derived geographic dataset is included under `public/assets/geography`. Data downloaded on 2026-10-28 were filtered to retain only currently unchanged features whose ways and all nodes were last edited by 2026-09-06 04:00 UTC. That produces an incomplete subset, not an authoritative historical snapshot: later-modified or deleted features are missing, and the actual 2026 construction state is not established by a map timestamp. Consult [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md) for the official airport plan, government road sources, photo references, provenance and other caveats.

`dist/nose-roads-review/index.html` compares 17 fixed-camera before/after images: front, quarter and side views of five families, plus two airport overviews. Open the game through `Start-ATC.cmd`; its bundled production assets do not require a live map connection. An optional development rebuild requires the usual Node.js/npm dependencies.

Validation includes the production build, 58 unit/simulation tests, four production-browser workflows, 17 paired game captures without browser errors, and explicit checks that rendered terminal-road chunks avoid runway and taxi corridors. The earlier 18 protected gameplay/UI/audio/airport/fleet source hashes remain unchanged. Performance and browser smoke results are recorded separately in `visual-qa/nose-roads-performance.json` and the `visual-qa/nose-roads-regression/` directory.

In a paired local Intel UHD 730 test at 1440×900 Medium, using 14 aircraft and two 180-frame samples per version, the average of the normal-speed median frame times changed from 21.05 ms to 21.75 ms (about 3.3% longer). This is one machine and scenario, not a universal FPS guarantee. Merging adjacent safe road chunks reduced the measured scene from roughly 322,777 triangles in the first implementation to 304,203; the preserved build measured 297,170.
